# Privacy

End O'Clock collects **nothing**.

- No analytics, tracking, accounts, or servers. The extension never sends data anywhere.
- Your settings and the current session (start and end time, what you said you'd do next) are saved only in your browser's local extension storage, on your computer.
- It runs only on `https://www.youtube.com/*`. It reads the page (and YouTube's own data about videos on that page) to know how long videos are. That never leaves your browser.
- Fonts and images are bundled inside the extension; nothing is downloaded from other sites.

## Permissions, in plain words

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Access to youtube.com | To show the start screen, hide what doesn't fit, and end sessions. |
| `storage` | To remember your settings and the current session on this computer. |
| `alarms` | To end a session on time even if you closed every YouTube tab. |
| Finding open YouTube tabs | When End O'Clock is installed or updated, YouTube tabs that were already open reload so it takes effect right away. |

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.
